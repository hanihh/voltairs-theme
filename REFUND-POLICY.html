We want you to love your Voltairs kit. If it is not right for you, you have 100 days from the date your order is delivered to request a return for a full refund.

Returns you can actually test

This guarantee is meant to be used. Pack the bags, run the pump, take the kit on a trip. If it does not save you the space and the baggage fees we promise, send it back. We only ask that the kit is complete: both compression bags, the pump, and its accessories.

How to start a return

Email us at info@voltairs.com with your order number and a sentence about what did not work. We will reply with a return authorization, the return address, and instructions for sending the kit back. Please do not ship anything back before you have that authorization, as we cannot process unannounced returns.

Discounted and bundled orders

Kits bought on sale, with a discount code, or as part of a bundle are covered by the same 100-day guarantee. Your refund is the amount you actually paid. Gift cards are the only thing we cannot refund.

Damaged or wrong items

Please check your order when it arrives. If the kit is defective, damaged in transit, or not what you ordered, email info@voltairs.com right away with a photo and we will make it right, usually with a replacement shipped at our cost.

Exchanges

The fastest route is to return the kit you have and place a new order once your return is approved.

Refunds

Once your return arrives and we have checked it, we will email you to confirm the refund. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank or card issuer may need a few extra days to post it. If more than 15 business days have passed since we confirmed your refund and you still do not see it, contact us at info@voltairs.com.

Return shipping

For defective, damaged, or incorrect items, we cover return shipping. For change-of-mind returns within the 100 days, return shipping is yours to arrange, and we recommend a tracked service so the kit does not go missing on the way back.

Questions about any of this? Write to info@voltairs.com and a human will answer.
