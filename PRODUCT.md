# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Travelers who resent checked-bag fees and overpacking: carry-on-only flyers, families packing for kids, business travelers, festival-goers. They land on the store from ads or word of mouth, usually on a phone, skeptical of gimmicky travel gadgets. The job: believe the compression claim quickly, understand the kit, buy without friction.

## Product Purpose
Single-product Shopify store for the Voltairs Travel Vacuum Bag Kit ($89.99: two 22lb IPX8 compression bags + a multi-function electric pump). The product's entire pitch is a visible physical transformation: a pile of clothes becomes a flat bag, saving up to 60% space and the ~$56-per-flight checked-bag fee. Success = a homepage that makes that transformation feel real and gets visitors to the product page.

## Brand Personality
Clever & confident. Witty, assured, a little playful: the brand that says "walk straight past the baggage carousel" with a smirk. Practical smarts, not luxury polish and not hype. Tagline: "More Space, Less Stress."

## Anti-references
- Loud discount DTC: countdown timers, "50% OFF TODAY" badges, flashing urgency, popups (Wish/Temu energy).
- Generic AI landing page: centered headline on a flat/gradient canvas, icon-card grids, stock metric rows.
- Corporate & sterile: gray-blue everything, stock airport photos, no personality.
- Established copy rules: no em dashes, no fabricated social proof or review counts, hero carries no discount offer, details inline rather than hidden in accordions.

## Design Principles
1. **Show the compression, don't claim it.** The product's magic is visual; every key surface should let the before/after transformation do the persuading.
2. **Confident calm over urgency.** No manufactured scarcity. Persuasion comes from demonstration, savings math, and wit.
3. **Navy owns the canvas.** Committed color strategy: deep navy (#03275b) carries the brand surfaces; cream (#efeeeb) and amber (#fdc656) are the only accents. Amber is reserved for the one thing that should draw the eye.
4. **Wit lives in the copy, restraint in the chrome.** Playfulness comes from words and one strong motion idea per surface, not from badges, stickers, or decoration.
5. **Mobile-first, always.** Most visitors arrive on phones; every design decision is validated at 320-430px before desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA. Every animation (marquees, compression motion, rotating text) ships a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. Text on navy must hit 4.5:1 (cream on navy passes). Touch targets ≥44px.
